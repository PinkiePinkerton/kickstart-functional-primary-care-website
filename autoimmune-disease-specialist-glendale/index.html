Autoimmune Disease

A Systemic Approach to Immune Regulation

Self-tolerance

Clinical Perspectives on Root-Cause Resolution and Inflammatory Control in Glendale, AZ

Autoimmune disease is characterized by a fundamental loss of “self-tolerance,” where the immune system begins to attack healthy tissues. In the traditional medical model, these conditions, ranging from Rheumatoid Arthritis and Lupus to Psoriasis and Celiac Disease, are often treated as localized problems restricted to specific organs or joints. At KickStart Functional Primary Care, we recognize that while symptoms may manifest in different parts of the body, the root of the issue is a systemic imbalance of the immune system.

For our patients in the Phoenix metro area, we move beyond simply suppressing the immune system. Our goal is to identify the environmental and metabolic triggers that have led to immune dysfunction and restore the body’s natural regulatory balance.

The Limitations of the "Immune-Suppression" Model

Traditional care for autoimmune conditions is primarily focused on reducing the severity of “flares” through the use of immunosuppressants, biologics, or steroids. While these interventions can be life-saving in acute stages, they frequently fail to address why the immune system became reactive in the first place.

The Challenges of Traditional Autoimmune Care:

Siloed Specialty Care

Patients are often managed by multiple specialists who treat individual symptoms without a unified strategy to address the underlying systemic inflammation.

Side-Effect Management

Long-term reliance on immunosuppressants can leave patients vulnerable to secondary infections and metabolic complications.

The "Wait for a Flare" Narrative

Patients are often told there is nothing they can do between flares, leading to a reactive rather than proactive approach to their health.

The KickStart Protocol:

Restoring Immune Tolerance

We believe that to heal an autoimmune condition, you must address the “soil” in which the immune system lives. Our Glendale clinic utilizes advanced functional screening to identify the specific triggers, such as gut dysbiosis, chronic infections, or toxic load, that keep your immune system in a state of high alert.

The 30-Minute Autoimmune Deep Dive

Clinical resolution requires an intensive investigation into a patient’s history and biochemistry. Our Signature 30-Minute Consultations provide the time necessary to examine advanced markers of intestinal permeability (Leaky Gut), environmental sensitivities, and systemic inflammatory status that traditional 7-minute visits overlook.

1
