Our Mission

Clinical Perspectives on Mitochondrial Health and Neuro-Inflammation in Glendale, AZ

Chronic fatigue and cognitive impairment, commonly referred to as “brain fog,” are among the most pervasive and dismissed symptoms in modern medicine. At KickStart Functional Primary Care, we recognize that persistent exhaustion is not a “natural part of aging” or a simple consequence of a busy lifestyle. We specialize in identifying the cellular and systemic drivers of low energy for patients in the Phoenix metro area who have been told their basic lab work is “normal” yet continue to struggle with daily vitality.

Our clinical focus is on the restoration of mitochondrial function and the mitigation of neuro-inflammation, moving past superficial stimulants to address why your body’s “power plants” have failed.

The Limitations of the "Just Get More Rest" Sick-Care Model

In the traditional medical system, chronic fatigue is frequently treated as a psychological issue or a lifestyle failure. When standard blood tests for anemia or thyroid function return within “pathological ranges,” patients are often left without answers or, worse, prescribed stimulants or antidepressants to mask the underlying physiological drain.

The Challenges of Traditional Energy Management:

Pathological vs. Optimal Ranges

Traditional labs are designed to find disease, not to optimize health. A patient can be "clinically normal" while having cellular energy production that is functioning at only a fraction of its capacity.

The Stimulant Trap

Relying on caffeine, sugar, or prescriptions to "get through the day" creates a cycle of adrenal strain and systemic inflammation that further depletes long-term energy reserves.

Ignoring the "Why"

Traditional care rarely investigates the role of chronic low-grade infections, heavy metal load, or "leaky" cellular membranes in the persistence of fatigue.
