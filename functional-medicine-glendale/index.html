Beyond "Normal" Labs. Finding the Root Cause through Precision Biology.

Most patients come to KickStart Functional Primary Care because they are tired of being told their labs are "normal" while they feel anything but. Traditional medicine is designed to detect Pathology, disease that has already progressed far enough to require significant intervention.

Functional Medicine is different. We view your health as a dynamic biological system, not a collection of isolated symptoms. We utilize advanced diagnostics to identify the Cellular Imbalances that occur years before a permanent diagnosis. If your condition can be measured through biological labs, it can be impacted through functional restoration.

The "Normal Lab" Trap

In the standard medical model, a "normal" range is based on a bell curve of the average population, a population that is statistically becoming more unwell every year.

The Range vs. The Optimal

Being at the bottom of a "normal" range for markers like Thyroid, Vitamin D, or Testosterone isn't health; it’s a clinical warning sign.

The Diagnostic Ceiling

Traditional doctors often stop testing once a major disease is ruled out. We start testing to find out how to get you back to 100%.

Systems Biology

We don't just ask what is wrong; we ask why the system is failing to maintain its own equilibrium.

The Four Pillars of Functional Medicine in Glendale

At our Glendale clinic, Dr. Drew investigates the fundamental pathways of human biology. Because we treat the "Operating System," our approach is effective for nearly any condition driven by biological markers.

1

Cellular Energy & Glycemic Integrity (Fuel)

We look beyond simple blood sugar to investigate Insulin Sensitivity and C-Peptide levels. By restoring how your cells process fuel, we address metabolic stumbles, "Weight Loss Resistance," and the energy crashes that traditional primary care often misses.

2

Endocrine & Hormonal Synchronization (Communication)

Your hormones are your body’s communication network. We map the intricate dance between your Thyroid, Adrenals (Cortisol), and Sex Hormones. When these signals are clear and synchronized, brain fog lifts and vitality returns.

3

Immunological Balance & Systemic Load (Defense)

Chronic pain and systemic inflammation are often signs of an immune system that has lost its "brakes." We investigate gut-barrier integrity and inflammatory markers to calm the immune response, allowing the body to stop fighting itself and start repairing.

4

Intestinal Integrity & The Microbiome (The Foundation)

70% of your immune system lives in your gut. If your microbiome is out of balance, it manifests throughout the entire body. We use advanced stool and sensitivity testing to rebuild the "Biological Soil" required for every other system to thrive.
